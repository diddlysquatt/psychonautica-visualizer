# CYMA (`cyma`)

Cymatics: sound made visible. A temple of resonators seen from a slowly orbiting, tilting viewpoint. At the centre a hero composition (one great plate, nested jewel plates, a carillon of bells, or a porthole onto a conveyor of plates) floats over an engraved floor. Round it run eight rings of gongs, water bowls and liquid-metal plates, one ring per frequency band, from the highs on the inside to the great sub gongs outside. Every plate keeps real physics: Chladni figures in heaped sand, Faraday standing waves as coloured caustics, or a mirror surface heaving through its mode.

## How it moves with the music
- Kick: the plates jump, a sound wave travels out across the floor, and a pair of bands (in turn) re-forms its sand figures.
- Sub and bass: the spokes carry the sub; the smoothed bass lifts the light of the temple.
- The eight bands: each ring hovers and rocks with its own band and draws that band as a spectrum rose (petal count and depth follow its pitch and level); the dais round the centre is an eight-band meter; the floor's grooves light with the spectrum.
- Bars: the rings step one slot per bar; rails between them carry their band's waveform as light.
- Snare and hats: sparks and glints on the plates and rims.
- Builds: the figures re-form faster as the build rises.
- Drops: the rings spin like a combination lock and flip edge-on into a new arrangement as the front passes.

## Framings (Auto Show)
- far (`0.22`): the temple as a wheel of rings round the centre.
- home (`0.5`): the hero composition with the inner rings around it.
- close (`0.95`): the sand figures and engraving of the centre plate; kept brief.
- kaleidoscope: folds in about 50% of visits; best folds 12 and 16; acceptable 6 and 8. A folded visit may glide on into a second fold.

## Cost
Light (`cost: 1`). Native quality and above (`#if HD>=1`) add finer detail to the plates; the higher tiers add steps to the water and sand figures, so a bowl shows a finer figure at each tier rather than the same one sharpened.

## Known weak points
- Warm palettes in loud passages stay in one hue family (red, orange, pink) across floor and plates.
- Highs and mids drive the metal and sand plates less than the other inputs.
- At a steep tilt, pale sound-field lines and glints cross the upper far field.
- The centre floor is one colour family over its whole disc, broken only by accent tiles and the plates.
