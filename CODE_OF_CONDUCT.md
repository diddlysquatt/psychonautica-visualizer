# Code of conduct

This is a place for making beautiful, strange things together. Think of it as a party someone set out
chairs and lights for: come as you are, and help keep it good for the next person.

- Be kind and assume good faith. People here range from first-timers to experts, and many work through
  coding agents. All of that is welcome.
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This washes out when the music gets loud" helps. Mockery does not.
- No harassment, hate, sexual attention nobody asked for, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Share only what is yours to share: original scenes, and nothing that hurts or deceives the people who
  run it.
- Take care with the subject. Not everyone here uses psychedelics, and nobody should feel pushed to.
  Do not offer or seek illegal goods here.

If something is wrong, tell the maintainers: open an issue, or for something private use the "Report"
option on the post or the contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions and block people who make the space worse, and will say why.
